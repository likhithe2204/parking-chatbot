# About Hyderabad Central Parking

Hyderabad Central Parking is a covered, multi-level car park in Madhapur, Hyderabad, built for daily commuters and visitors to the HITEC City business district.

The parking has three zones on three floors: Zone A on the ground floor, Zone B on the first floor and Zone C on the second floor. Together they hold 180 spaces.

Every floor of Hyderabad Central Parking has CCTV cameras, bright lighting and clear signs. Security staff are stationed at the gate, and the cameras are watched around the clock.

A backup generator keeps the lights, the barriers and the lifts working during power cuts.

Opening hours, prices and the number of free spaces are kept in the live reservation system. The assistant looks them up for each question and never guesses them.

The assistant can answer questions about the parking, check prices, opening hours and free spaces, and collect a reservation request for the administrator to approve.

This is fictional demo content created for a training project. It does not describe a real business.
