# Payments

Parking is paid at the facility, not in the chat. The assistant does not take any payment.

Hyderabad Central Parking accepts UPI, debit and credit cards, and contactless payment at the payment kiosks.

The assistant never asks for card numbers, CVV codes, UPI PINs or one-time passwords. If a message asks for these, it is not from the parking.

Card numbers, phone numbers and email addresses typed in the chat are masked by the assistant's privacy filter. Customers should not share them.

Prices depend on the vehicle type and are kept in the live pricing system. Ask the assistant for current prices. All prices are in Indian Rupees.

Receipts are available from the help desk at the facility.
