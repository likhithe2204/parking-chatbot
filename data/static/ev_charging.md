# EV Charging

Zone C, on the second floor of Hyderabad Central Parking, has the EV charging points. Zone C has 40 spaces.

The charging points suit common Indian electric cars that use the Type 2 AC and CCS2 standards.

Charging is billed separately from the parking fee. Customers confirm the charging fee at the facility.

EV charging points are only for electric vehicles that are charging. Other vehicles must not use them.

A reservation takes a space in a zone, not a specific charging point. The zone is assigned automatically, so a reservation does not guarantee a space in Zone C.

Customers who need EV charging should tell the parking office or the security staff in advance. To see how many Zone C spaces are free, ask for the free spaces in Zone C for your period.

Charging for electric two-wheelers is not available.
