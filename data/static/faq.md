# Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to create an account?** No. The assistant only needs your name, your car number and the period you want.

**Is my reservation confirmed straight away?** No. It is confirmed only after the human administrator approves it. Until then it is pending.

**How will I know the decision?** Ask the assistant for the status of your request. While the chat is open, it also announces the decision when it arrives.

**Can I park a two-wheeler?** Yes. Two-wheelers park in Zone A on the ground floor.

**Can I park a bus or a truck?** No. Buses, trucks and other large vehicles are not allowed.

**What is the maximum vehicle height?** The maximum vehicle height is 2.1 metres.

**Are there EV charging points?** Yes, in Zone C on the second floor. A reservation does not guarantee a Zone C space.

**How long can a reservation be?** Up to 30 days, and it must start in the future.

**Can I cancel for free?** Yes, up to 2 hours before the start time. Contact the parking office to cancel.

**Can I pay in the chat?** No. Payment is made at the facility. The assistant never asks for card or UPI details.

**What if my reservation is refused?** You can submit a new request for a different period. The administrator's reason, if any, is shown to you.

**Is the parking safe?** Yes. Every floor has CCTV, and security staff are at the gate.

**Is there a lift and step-free access?** Yes. The entrance is step-free and lifts serve every floor.

**Will the assistant share the administrator's phone number or email?** No. Private staff details are never shared.
