# Cancellations and Changes

Customers can cancel a reservation free of charge up to 2 hours before the start time.

A cancellation less than 2 hours before the start time may be charged. The administrator confirms the amount, and the assistant never invents charges.

The assistant cannot cancel or change a request that has been submitted. Customers should contact the parking office at the facility for cancellations and changes.

To change the dates of a booking, the simplest way is to cancel it and submit a new request. The new request gets its own request id and needs a new approval.

To extend a stay, submit a new reservation request for the extra period. An extension is not guaranteed and depends on free spaces and on the administrator's approval.

Staying longer than the booked period may be charged. The administrator decides the amount.
