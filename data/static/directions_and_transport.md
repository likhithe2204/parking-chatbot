# Directions and Transport

By car, drive along Hitech City Road in Madhapur to reach Hyderabad Central Parking at Plot 12. The entrance is on the main road, next to the Cyber Towers signal.

By metro, get off at Hitec City station. It is the nearest metro station to Hyderabad Central Parking, about a 5 minute walk from the entrance.

By bus, city buses stop along Hitech City Road close to the parking. Ask the security staff at the gate for the current routes.

By cab or auto, drivers can drop off or pick up customers at the bay next to the entrance. The bay is only for drop-off and pick-up, not for waiting or parking.

Places near Hyderabad Central Parking include Cyber Towers, the HITEC City business district, Inorbit Mall and Shilparamam. All are within a short walk or auto ride.

Hitech City Road is busiest on weekday mornings and evenings. Allow extra time to reach the entrance so you arrive before your reservation starts.

On arrival, security staff at the gate can check your car number against the approved reservations.
