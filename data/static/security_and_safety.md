# Security and Safety

Every floor of Hyderabad Central Parking has CCTV cameras, and security staff are stationed at the gate.

The parking has bright lighting, marked emergency exits and fire safety equipment on every floor.

A backup generator keeps the lights and the lifts working during power cuts.

In an emergency, tell the nearest security staff member at once. For fire, police or medical emergencies, call 112, the Indian emergency number.

Lost property is handed to the security staff. Customers can ask the parking office about items they have lost.

The assistant never shares a customer's reservation details, car number or contact details with anyone else.
