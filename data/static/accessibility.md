# Accessibility

Hyderabad Central Parking has a step-free entrance with a gentle ramp, and lifts that serve every floor.

Accessible parking bays are on the ground floor, in Zone A, close to the lifts.

The assistant cannot reserve an accessible bay yet. Customers who need one should tell the security staff at the gate when they arrive, or contact the parking office in advance.

Security staff can help customers with directions and mobility equipment during their duty hours.

Accessible bays are for drivers who need them. Please leave them free for those who do.
