# Parking Rules

Customers of Hyderabad Central Parking must follow the instructions of the security staff at all times.

Park only inside the marked bay lines. Do not block lanes, exits, lifts or other vehicles.

Drive slowly inside the building, at walking pace, and switch the engine off when the vehicle is parked.

Smoking and open flames are not allowed anywhere in the parking.

Pets must stay inside the vehicle at all times.

Do not leave valuables in view. Hyderabad Central Parking is not responsible for belongings left in vehicles.

Electric vehicle charging points are only for vehicles that are charging. Accessible bays are only for drivers who need them.

Report any damage, incident or suspicious activity to the security staff straight away.
