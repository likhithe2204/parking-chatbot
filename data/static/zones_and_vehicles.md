# Zones and Vehicles

Zone A is on the ground floor of Hyderabad Central Parking and has 60 spaces. It is used for short stays and for two-wheelers.

Zone B is on the first floor and has 80 spaces. It is used for regular car parking.

Zone C is on the second floor and has 40 spaces. It has EV charging points for electric vehicles.

Hyderabad Central Parking accepts cars, electric cars and two-wheelers. Two-wheelers park in Zone A.

Buses, trucks and other large vehicles are not allowed. The maximum vehicle height is 2.1 metres.

When the administrator approves a reservation, the system assigns a zone automatically. It picks the zone with the most free spaces for the booked period.

Customers cannot choose a zone through the assistant. To know if there is room, ask for free spaces for your period, for example from 2026-12-10 10:00 to 2026-12-10 14:00.

Free spaces are counted per zone and per period. A booking only reduces the free spaces during its own period, not at other times.
